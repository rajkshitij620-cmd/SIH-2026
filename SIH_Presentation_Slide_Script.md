# 🎙️ TourMitra — SIH Technical Approach Presentation (90-Second Pitch)

> **Presentation Target Duration:** 1 minute 15 seconds to 1 minute 25 seconds (~200 words at a steady, confident pace).  
> **Slide:** *Technical Approach, Architecture & App Workflow (Smart India Hackathon 2026)*

---

## ⏱️ Live Presentation Script (Word-for-Word)

### **[0:00 – 0:20] Hook & Architecture Overview**
> *"Good morning, respected judges. To solve real-world tourism challenges, we built **TourMitra** using a robust, modular, and scalable full-stack architecture.*  
> 
> *On the frontend, we use **React with Vite and TailwindCSS** for a lightning-fast, mobile-first experience, integrating **WebRTC** for live photo uploads and **HTML5 Geolocation** for instant position tracking.*  
> 
> *Our backend runs on **FastAPI and Python 3.11**, offering high-performance asynchronous endpoints secured with **JWT, Bcrypt, and Role-Based Access Control**."*

---

### **[0:20 – 0:50] Core AI & Smart Integrations**
> *(Point to Section 3 & 4 on the left)*  
> 
> *"At the heart of TourMitra is our AI engine. We combine **TF-IDF with Cosine Similarity** to match travellers and dynamically redirect footfall away from congested hotspots to balanced hidden gems.*  
> 
> *For accurate guidance, we use a **RAG-powered GPT-4o-mini pipeline** that eliminates hallucinations, along with **Multilingual NLP** supporting all 22 official Indian languages.*  
> 
> *We also integrate **OpenWeatherMap** to automatically swap outdoor activities for indoor cultural spots during bad weather, **MapTiler with OpenStreetMap** for interactive navigation, and **MongoDB Atlas** for scalable cloud storage."*

---

### **[0:50 – 1:25] Methodology, Workflow & Real Impact**
> *(Point to the Flowchart on the right)*  
> 
> *"Looking at our workflow: Once a user signs in and plans a trip, they can choose between **Solo, Family, or Group travel**.*  
> 
> *For solo travellers, our smart matching algorithm connects verified travellers heading to the same destination. Once accepted, it creates a dedicated **Group Chat** and generates a **unified Tour Guide** with shared budgeting.*  
> 
> *As seen in our prototype outcomes, this directly delivers **over 4 hours of queue time saved**, zero weather disruptions, and connects tourists directly to **local micro-businesses**."*

---

### **[1:25 – 1:30] Punchy Closing**
> *"In summary, TourMitra turns unpredictable trips into personalized, stress-free, and crowd-balanced journeys. Thank you!"*

## 💡 Practical Speaker Tips for Hackathon Judging

1. **Keep Eye Contact:** Don't read continuously from the screen. Glance at the left column when talking about tech stack, then shift your hand towards the flowchart on the right when explaining the user journey.
2. **Highlight the "Why", not just "What":** Instead of just saying *"We used OpenWeatherMap"*, say *"We used OpenWeatherMap to adapt itineraries automatically during rain"*. Judges love impact-driven technical decisions!
3. **Pacing:** Speak naturally like you're walking a friend through how your product works.
